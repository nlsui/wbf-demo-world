Beneath the stilts, the tidal flats hide a maze of channels that only flat-bottomed skiffs can run, and only at the lowest ebb. Untaxed salt and spice come in this way on moonless nights.

A few of [[9461d453-b5e5-4eee-8d75-6647863d2aab|the Tidewardens]] are paid to let the beacons gutter at the right hour. The rest believe the stories about drowned lights on the flats.
